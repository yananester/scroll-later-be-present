Privacy,
plainly stated.

An app about being present shouldn't be watching you. Here's exactly what we do and don't do with your information.

Effective May 26, 2026
The short version
  • Your app selections and schedules stay on your device.
  • We don't track which apps you use or when.
  • We don't sell, share, or rent your data. Ever.
  • We don't show ads and we don't profile you.
  • If you email us, we keep that email so we can reply.

What this policy covers

This Privacy Policy describes how Family O’Clock ("we," "us," or "the app") handles information when you use our mobile application. By using Family O’Clock, you agree to the practices described here.

Information we don't collect

Family O’Clock is designed around the principle that we don't need to know much about you to help you. Specifically, we do not collect:

  • Your name, address, or phone number
  • Your location
  • Which apps you actually open or how long you use them
  • The content of anything you do inside other apps
  • Your contacts, photos, or messages
  • Advertising identifiers for tracking across apps

Information stored on your device

When you use Family O’Clock, the following information is stored locally on your iPhone:

  • Your reminder schedules — the days and times you've chosen for gentle pauses
  • Your app selections — which apps you've chosen to pause (stored using Apple's Screen Time framework, which keeps this data private even from us)
  • App preferences — settings you configure inside Family O’Clock

This information stays on your device. It's not uploaded to our servers, and we have no way to see it.

Apple Screen Time permission

Family O’Clock uses Apple's Family Controls and Screen Time APIs to offer gentle pauses on the apps you select. Apple deliberately designed these APIs so that the apps you choose are never revealed to us — not their names, not their icons, nothing. Only your device knows which apps you've selected. We just ask iOS to gently pause them during your scheduled windows.

Information we receive when you contact us

If you email us at ynester.bs@gmail.com, we'll receive your email address and whatever you choose to share in your message. We use this information only to respond to you and to improve the app. We keep these emails as long as is reasonably useful for support purposes.

App Store and subscription information

If Family O’Clock offers paid features, purchases are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We receive aggregated, anonymous purchase information from Apple (for example, how many subscriptions were active in a given period). We don't receive your payment details, name, or Apple ID.

To manage or cancel a subscription, go to Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions on your device. Refund requests are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Crash and diagnostic data

If you've opted in to share diagnostic data with app developers in your iOS settings, Apple may send us anonymized crash reports to help us fix bugs. These reports do not contain personal information. You can change this setting anytime in Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements.

Third parties

Family O’Clock does not use third-party analytics, advertising networks, or tracking SDKs. We don't share your information with anyone because we don't have it to share.

Children's privacy

Family O’Clock is intended for adults — typically parents who want to be more present with their families. The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with information, please contact us so we can delete it.

Your rights

Because almost everything stays on your device, you control your own data directly:

  • To view your data: open the app — that's where it lives
  • To delete your data: delete the app, and everything goes with it
  • To request deletion of support emails: email us at ynester.bs@gmail.com and we'll remove your messages from our inbox

If you're in a region with data protection laws like GDPR (Europe) or CCPA (California), you have the right to access, correct, or delete the limited information we have about you (essentially, your support emails). Contact us to exercise these rights.

Changes to this policy

If we ever change this policy, we'll update the effective date at the top of this page. If the changes are significant, we'll do our best to let you know inside the app before they take effect.

Contact

Questions about this policy or how Family O’Clock handles your information? Email ynester.bs@gmail.com. A real person will read it.
